### Spirantix Concierge: how to talk with it

This page is written to be read aloud, and to be copied and pasted. It explains how to talk with the
Spirantix Concierge and what it can do for you.

### You can talk instead of type

You do not have to type. You can speak your question and it appears in the box. Then you send it the
same way you always do. The concierge always offers you a few clear choices for what to do next, so
you can simply say "the first one" or "number two".

**On a phone or tablet:** tap the microphone on your keyboard, speak normally, then send.
**On a computer:** click where you type, press the Windows key and the H key together, speak, then
press them together again to stop. On a Mac, press the microphone key or the dictation key.

If it does not work, ask: "How do I talk instead of type?" The concierge walks you through it on the
device you are using.

### What it is

The Spirantix Concierge is one assistant you talk with in a chat. It remembers the people, dates,
events, stories, reminders, photos, and belongings that matter to you and your family, and it answers
your questions about them.

There is only one assistant. You never have to choose who you are talking to.

### How to start

1. Open the link you were given. It opens a chat.
2. Say hello, then ask in your own words.
3. If you are not sure where to begin, tap one of the four buttons, or simply say what you need.

You do not need to learn any special words. Ask the way you would ask a friend.

### The four doors

The four buttons are four doors. Each one starts you in the right place.

**Remember: people, places, dates, events**
Use this door to ask about something the concierge already knows. It can tell you about a person, a
day, a trip, a reminder you set, or an item you recorded. It answers only from what you have told it,
and it says plainly when it does not know.

**Capture: stories, reminders, events, items, documents**
Use this door to give the concierge something new to keep. It saves a story you tell it, sets a
reminder, records a belonging, and adds an event to your calendar. To add a document, a receipt, or a
picture, it shows you the Spirantix page and walks you through it.

**Photos: images, albums, stories**
Use this door to work with your photos. It can show you a picture, make an album, add a caption, and
record the names of the people in a photo when you tell it who they are. It keeps names, captions and
small previews. Your full pictures stay in your own library.

A note on the word **seal**: a seal is a small mark on a file that shows it has not changed since it
was kept. You can ask whether a seal is good. Making a seal comes later, in the app, and the
concierge never promises a sealed copy in the chat.

**Update: reminders, appointments, times**
Use this door when something has changed. Mark a reminder as done, remove a reminder you no longer
need, change the time of a reminder, or change or cancel an appointment. Before it changes anything,
it says the exact item, day, and time back to you, waits for your yes, then reads it back so you can
confirm it.

### Things you do with a sentence, not a button

**Ask about a seal.** Say: "Is my seal still good?" The concierge tells you what is recorded about the
seal on an item, in plain words. Making a seal happens in the app, later.

**Add a person who can help you.** A helper is a family member or caregiver you choose. Permission to add calendar events does not by itself allow that person to view your calendar. You can separately allow the helper to view your full daily event calendar. You can remove access at any time.

**Hear any answer read aloud.** Every answer can be spoken. If your device has voice turned on, the
answer is read to you. If you want to hear it again, ask: "Read that to me."

### What it will not do

- It does not give medical or legal advice. It asks you to speak with a doctor or a lawyer.
- It does not recognize faces in this release. It cannot tell who someone is from a picture. It uses
  only the names and notes you have already given it.
- It cannot start a conversation on its own. It waits for you to speak first.
- It cannot record audio inside the chat. You record a voice story on the Spirantix page, and it
  helps you attach it.
- It does not make a seal in this release. You can ask about a seal; making one comes in the app.

### When it does not know

It says "I don't know" in plain words, then tells you what it can do instead, and offers you the next
step. It never guesses and it never fills in a likely answer.

### The phases

**Phase 1, the ChatGPT plugin.** You talk with the concierge in ChatGPT, and you have Spirantix
account pages for your people, your photos and albums, your voice stories, your reminders and your
items. Sealing a document is not in this phase.

**Phase 2, the Claude plugin.** The same concierge and the same records, reachable from Claude. Same
account, same data, same voice.

**Phase 3, the app.** The app becomes the main place to use Spirantix, on a tablet or a phone. It adds
reading everything aloud screen by screen, the community sponsor role, paid storage, read-only library
sharing, and sealing a document. Sponsor access is separate from helper access: a connected senior living center may add events for its connected members, but must not view their calendars.
