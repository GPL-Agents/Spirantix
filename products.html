What it does for you today

Six everyday things you can do

No special words to learn. Ask the way you would ask a friend.

People

Names, birthdays, who someone is, how you know them. Ask "Who is my granddaughter?" and it answers from what you told it.

Calendar

Appointments and a calendar you can ask about: today, this week, what is next.

Reminders

Reminders for anything you have to do. They arrive by text or email. A phone call that reads your day is on the way.

Photos and memories

Tell it about the lake, the old house, your first job, and it keeps your words. Ask who is in a picture and it answers from the name you saved. Organize photos into albums and give an album a voice story.

Valuables and keepsakes

The watch, the rocking chair, the locket. Where it is kept and who it belonged to.

Safe, ad-free web search

Ask it to look something up. No ads, no sponsored results, and it always names the site the answer came from.

Plug-ins versus the app

What is where

The plug-ins and the app share one account. The app adds what a chat window cannot do.

In the ChatGPT and Claude plug-ins

  • Everything on the full list, inside the chat you already use
  • One sign-in for both, one set of memories
  • No separate app to open

In the Spirantix Concierge App

  • Everything above, without ChatGPT or Claude
  • Screens built for a tablet or phone
  • Adding photos and documents inside the app
  • Exporting or deleting your account in the app
  • Choosing where reminders reach you

Reminders go by text and by email, whichever the member turns on.

Safety first

What the Concierge will not do

  • A reminder notifies the member when its time comes. An email is read back to you, recipient and words, before it goes out.
  • It will not make up a photo. If there is none on record, it says so.
  • It will not invent a name or a date. It answers from what you told it, and says when it does not know.
  • It will not change anything without saying the exact item, day, and time back to you first.
  • It is not a doctor and not an emergency service. Call 911 in an emergency.
  • Your records stay in your account, and you can ask for them back or ask for them to be deleted.
