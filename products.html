The Spirantix Concierge is one friendly assistant. You talk in your own words. It keeps the people, dates, stories, and reminders that matter to you and your family, and it answers when you ask.

What it does for you today

Six everyday things you can do

No special words to learn. Ask the way you would ask a friend.

Remember the people in your life

Names, birthdays, who someone is, how you know them. Ask "Who is my granddaughter?" and it answers from what you told it.

Keep your stories

Tell it about the lake, the old house, your first job. It keeps your words, so you can ask for them again any time.

Hold on to your photos

Ask to see a picture and it shows what is on record for that person or event. It can make an album and add a caption you give it.

Keep your days straight

Reminders, appointments, and a calendar you can ask about. "What is on my calendar today?" "What is coming up this week?"

Record what you own

The watch, the rocking chair, the locket. Where it is kept and who it belonged to, so the story stays with the thing.

Go at your own pace

You can speak instead of type, and have answers read aloud. It offers a few clear choices instead of long lists.

Plug-ins versus the app

What is where

The two plug-ins run the same Concierge on the same account. The app adds the parts a chat window cannot do.

In the ChatGPT and Claude plug-ins

  • Remembering people, dates, and events
  • Stories, memories, and answers read aloud
  • Photos, albums, and captions
  • Reminders, appointments, and the calendar
  • Belongings and what you own
  • One sign-in for both, one set of memories

In the Spirantix app

  • Everything above, without ChatGPT or Claude
  • Screens built for a tablet or phone
  • Adding photos and documents inside the app
  • Exporting or deleting your account in the app
  • Choosing where reminders reach you

Text reminders are built and waiting on carrier approval, so they are switched off for now.

Safety first

What the Concierge will not do

  • It will not send email or text messages to other people, and it will not make calls for you.
  • It will not make up a photo. If there is no picture on record, it says so plainly.
  • It will not invent a name or a date. It answers from what you told it, and says when it does not know.
  • It will not change anything without saying the exact item, day, and time back to you first.
  • It is not a doctor and not an emergency service. Call 911 in an emergency.
  • Your records stay in your account, and you can ask for them back or ask for them to be deleted.
