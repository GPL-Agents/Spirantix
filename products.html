For families & caregivers

Stay close. Help when invited.

Share the everyday moments and family stories that keep you connected. Offer practical help with the access your family member chooses.

  • Share photos, albums, and memories with the people you choose.
  • Help with plans and reminders when permission is granted. Adding an event does not automatically let you read the calendar.
  • Keep important names, relationships, and stories together.
  • Choose access to photos and memories, change permissions, or remove access as needs change.

The complete feature list

Everything in your Concierge

One account for your daily life, personal stories, and connections.

People & relationships

  • Names, birthdays, relationships, and notes about the people you know.
  • Friends, family, and caregiver connections, with permissions you control.
  • Let a trusted helper add plans or reminders. Separately allow an in-app notice if a reminder is missed.

Calendar & reminders

  • Ask about today, this week, and what is next.
  • Create plans and reminders, and choose text or email notifications.
  • Connect Google Calendar and choose the calendars you use.

Photos, memories & sharing

  • Keep stories in your own words; find them again by asking.
  • Organize photos into albums, label people, and correct captions.
  • Record a voice story for an album.
  • Share photos, albums, and memories with selected people.

Belongings & family history

  • Keep details about valuables and keepsakes, including where they are stored.
  • Save who an item belonged to and the story behind it.
  • Add supporting photos and documents.

Conversation & helpful answers

  • Ask questions in plain language, speak instead of typing, and have text read aloud.
  • Search the web without sponsored results, with sources you can visit.
  • Ask the Concierge to send email to saved contacts, with confirmation before sending.

Access & personal control

  • Use the app on a phone, tablet, or laptop.
  • Manage connected people, apps, and reminder preferences.
  • Export or delete your account from the app.
