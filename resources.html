AI tool highlights

Good places to begin with AI

These general-purpose assistants can explain ideas, help with writing, organize plans, work with images, and answer follow-up questions in a conversational way.

ChatGPT

Use text or voice to ask questions, explore ideas, draft writing, compare options, and get everyday help.

Explore ChatGPT

Claude

Ask questions, work through ideas, simplify complex information, analyze documents, and create written material.

Explore Claude

Gemini

Google’s AI assistant can help with writing, learning, planning, images, and tasks connected to Google services.

Explore Gemini

Microsoft Copilot

An AI assistant for questions, research, writing, and creating content, with additional features inside Microsoft 365 apps.

Explore Copilot

Use AI as a helper, not a final authority

AI tools can make mistakes. Verify important health, legal, financial, and safety information with a qualified professional or an authoritative source, and avoid sharing sensitive personal information.

A broader directory of tools and services

Below the highlights is a wider list of resources grouped by topic, from AI classes and memory support to photo, story, and heirloom services, along with ways to check whether a photo or document is authentic. Companionship, memory support, monitoring, and daily-living services for older adults, families, and care organizations.

Authenticity

Document and photo authenticity

Standards and services that help confirm where a photo, video, or document came from and whether it has been changed.

C2PA

An open technical standard for recording where a photo, video, or document came from and whether it has been changed. Free for anyone to use.

Visit website

Content Authenticity Initiative

An industry group that promotes the C2PA standard and tools that show the source and history of digital content.

Visit website

Truepic

A company that authenticates photos and videos at the moment they are captured, using the C2PA standard. Sold mainly to businesses such as insurers and lenders.

Visit website

Numbers Protocol

A service that records the origin and history of digital media on a blockchain. Offers free and paid developer plans.

Visit website

OriginStamp

A service that creates a verifiable timestamp for documents and files, proving when they existed. Pay per use.

Visit website

How we choose

We favor websites, tools, and apps with clear everyday uses, approachable experiences, responsible practices, and genuine value for older adults. A listing is not a guarantee of every article, feature, answer, product, or service offered by an outside organization.
