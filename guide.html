Spirantix Concierge: how to talk with it

Written to be read aloud, and to copy and paste. It explains how to talk with the Concierge and what it can do.

You can talk instead of type

You do not have to type. Speak your question and it appears in the box. Then send it as usual. It offers a few clear choices, so you can say "the first one" or "number two".

On a phone or tablet: tap the microphone on your keyboard, speak normally, then send. On a computer: click where you type, press the Windows key and the H key together, speak, then press them together again to stop. On a Mac, press the microphone key or the dictation key.

If it does not work, ask: "How do I talk instead of type?" The concierge walks you through it on the device you are using.

What it is

One assistant in a chat. It remembers your people, dates, stories, reminders, photos, and belongings, and answers your questions about them.

There is only one assistant. You never choose who you are talking to.

How to start

  1. Open the link you were given. It opens a chat.
  2. Say hello, then ask in your own words.
  3. If you are not sure where to begin, tap one of the four buttons, or simply say what you need.

You do not need to learn any special words. Ask the way you would ask a friend.

The four doors

Four buttons, four doors. Each one starts you in the right place.

Remember: people, places, dates, events Ask about something it already knows: a person, a day, a trip, a reminder, an item. It answers only from what you told it, and says when it does not know.

Capture: stories, reminders, events, items, documents Give it something new to keep: a story, a reminder, a belonging, a calendar event. To add a document, a receipt, or a picture, it opens the Spirantix page and walks you through it.

Photos: images, albums, stories Work with your photos: show a picture, make an album, add a caption, record who is in it. It keeps names, captions, and small previews. Your full pictures stay in your own library.

Update: reminders, appointments, times Use this door when something changed. Mark a reminder done, remove one, change a time, or change or cancel an appointment. Before it changes anything it says the exact item, day, and time back to you, waits for your yes, then reads it back.

Things you do with a sentence, not a button

Hear any answer read aloud. Every answer can be spoken. If your device has voice turned on, the answer is read to you. If you want to hear it again, ask: "Read that to me."

Family and caregivers

A helper is a family member or caregiver you choose. Only you can add or remove one, and you can take it back at any time.

To add a helper:

  1. In the app, open My helpers and choose Add a helper.
  2. Type their email address. They need their own Spirantix account first.
  3. Under What can this person do?, choose what they can do for you: add to your to-do list, add to your calendar, or get a notice inside Spirantix when you miss a reminder.
  4. Choose Add this helper. Their name then shows under My helpers.

What a helper can do:

  • Only what you chose. Everything you did not choose stays closed to them.
  • Adding an event for you does not let them read your calendar.
  • The missed-reminder notice is inside Spirantix only. It is not a phone call, not a text message, and not an email.
  • They cannot add anyone else as a helper.

To stop it, open My helpers and choose Remove. It takes effect right away. You can have up to 10 helpers.

Your community

If you belong to a senior living community or a community group, you can choose it in Spirantix. Pick your community from the list, or add it when it is not there yet. Your community manager approves you, and from then on your community is part of your Spirantix.

What your community brings you:

  • Community activities appear on your calendar, next to your own appointments.
  • You can sign up for an activity right from your calendar.
  • You can add a reminder for the activities that interest you most, so you hear about them in time.
  • Photos from activities you went to arrive in a shared album from your community manager, so the day is kept with your memories.

It is an easy way to stay in touch with the people around you.

What it will not do

  • It gives no medical or legal advice. It asks you to speak with a doctor or a lawyer.
  • It does not recognize faces in this release. It cannot tell who someone is from a picture. It uses only the names and notes you have already given it.
  • It cannot start a conversation on its own. It waits for you to speak first.
  • It cannot record audio inside the chat. You record a voice story on the Spirantix page, and it helps you attach it.

When it does not know

It says "I don't know" in plain words, tells you what it can do instead, and offers the next step. It never guesses.

The phases

Phase 1, the ChatGPT plugin. You talk with the Concierge in ChatGPT, with Spirantix pages for people, photos, albums, voice stories, reminders, and items.

Phase 2, the Claude plugin. Same Concierge, same records, reached from Claude.

Phase 3, the app. The app becomes the main place to use Spirantix, on a tablet or phone. It adds read-aloud screen by screen, the community sponsor role, paid storage, and read-only library sharing. Sponsor access is separate from helper access: a connected senior living community may add events for its members, but must not view their calendars.
