A clear privacy boundary

Sponsoring the app does not open a resident’s account.

Community calendars and content can come into the resident’s app. Their personal conversations, stories, and activity do not flow back to the sponsor. Family and caregiver access follows the permissions granted in the app.

A focused alternative to a social feed

Community photos and shared stories have a place beside the resident’s personal memories. Spirantix keeps that experience focused on the people and content they choose, without an advertising feed.

Facebook provides feed controls and recommendations, and includes advertising. Google Search can show labeled sponsored placements within or alongside AI responses. Spirantix’s web search presents answers without sponsored results and names its sources.
