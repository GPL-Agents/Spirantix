You can talk instead of type. Speak your question and it appears in the box, then send it the way you always do.

  • On a Windows computer: click in the box where you type, press the Windows key and the H key together, and say your question. Press them together again to stop.
  • On a Mac: press the Fn (function) key twice to start Dictation, or press the microphone key on the keyboard, and say your question.
  • On a phone or tablet: tap the microphone on your keyboard and say your question.

How to use

You do not need to learn any special words. Ask the way you would ask a friend.

What it is

The Spirantix Concierge is a helper you talk with in a chat. It remembers the people, photos, stories, and belongings your family tells it about, and it answers your questions about them.

What to say first

  1. Open the link you were given. It opens a chat.
  2. Say hello, then ask your question in your own words.
  3. If you are not sure where to begin, tap one of the four buttons, or simply say what you need.

One concierge, four kinds of help

You never have to choose who you are talking to. There is only one helper, and it takes you where you need to go.

  • Remember -- ask about a person, a day, a trip, a reminder, or an item you already told it about.
  • Capture -- give it something new to keep: a story, a reminder, a calendar event, or an item.
  • Photos -- show, organize, and label photos and albums. Your full pictures stay in your own library.
  • Update -- mark a reminder done, remove one, change its time or wording, or change or cancel a calendar event.

What it remembers

  • The people in your family and what you tell it about them
  • Photos and albums, labeled with the names you give them
  • Voice stories attached to a photo, an album, or an item
  • Reminders you set
  • Items you record, along with their story
  • Events you add to your calendar

What it will not do yet

  • It does not give medical or legal advice. It will ask you to speak with a doctor or a lawyer.
  • It does not recognize faces in this first release. It can only use names and notes you have already given it.
  • It cannot start a conversation on its own. It waits for you to speak first.
  • It cannot record audio inside the chat. You record a voice story on your own phone or on our page, and it helps you attach it.
  • It does not seal a document in this first release. Sealing comes later, in the web app.
  • To add events or activities we need to connect your calendar. Do you want to connect a Google account now?
A second way to reach your concierge is on the way. Spirantix is building a second assistant surface. This page will be updated with details when it is ready.

Supported prompts

Copy, paste, and change the details to match your family

These are real examples the Concierge understands today.

Remember a person

  • "Tell me about my daughter Sophie."
  • "Who is in my family?"
  • "What did we do last Thanksgiving?"

Remember an item

  • "Keep track of this watch. My grandmother gave it to me."
  • "What did my grandfather own?"
  • "What do you know about the wooden clock?"
  • "Add my grandmother's ring to my list."

Capture a memory or story

  • "Remember that Sophie learned to ride her bike at the lake."
  • "Save the story of how we met."
  • "What stories have I told you about our trip to Maine?"
  • "Add a note that this recipe came from Aunt Rosa."

Set a reminder

  • "Remind me to take my pills at eight tonight."
  • "Remind me to call my brother tomorrow morning."
  • "I took my pills, you can mark that done."
  • "Add my hair appointment to my calendar."

Ask about a photo or album

  • "Show me pictures of Sophie."
  • "What albums do I have?"
  • "Make an album of our trip."
  • "This is my daughter Sophie in this photo."

Find something you stored

  • "What is on my calendar today?"
  • "What reminders do I have?"
  • "What documents do I have for the painting?"
  • "Make me a list of everything I have recorded."

Questions about using the Concierge

Answers for a first-time user or their family

Looking for a question about lessons, classes, or the website instead? Visit the Questions page.

Do I need a ChatGPT account to use the Concierge?

Yes. You will need a ChatGPT account and a Spirantix account, and the Concierge asks you to connect the two the first time you use it.

Is there a cost to use the Concierge?

There is no charge to use the Concierge today.

Can it change or cancel something already on my calendar?

To add events or activities we need to connect your calendar. Do you want to connect a Google account now?

Will it recognize people in my photos automatically?

No. It does not identify a person from a photo in this first release. It can only use the names and notes you have already given it.

Is a document sealed or certified when I add it?

No. Nothing is sealed when you add a document in this first release, and there is no seal yet to check. Sealing comes later, in the web app.

Can someone else help me use my account?

Yes. On your Spirantix web account, you can add a named helper by email. That person can add reminders and add calendar events for you, and you can remove them at any time.

Can the helper see everything I have stored?

No. A helper can only add reminders and add calendar events. Looking at your library is not part of this first release.

How will a reminder actually reach me?

Reminder delivery is planned through email with a calendar file attached, a Google Calendar event, or a text message. Acknowledging a reminder is a separate action you take with the Concierge.

Can I add a voice story?

Yes. You record it on your own phone or on the Spirantix page, and the Concierge walks you through attaching it to a photo, an album, or an item. It cannot record audio inside the chat itself.

Does it give medical or legal advice?

No. It will ask you to speak with a doctor or a lawyer.

What happens if it does not know the answer?

It says so plainly and lets the Spirantix team know, so the answer can be added later.

Is my information private?

Your conversation is processed through ChatGPT's infrastructure. Read the Spirantix privacy policy for details on how information is handled.

Does it work when I am offline?

Not yet. Offline mode is not available in this first release.
