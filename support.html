What the Concierge app is.

Spirantix is a private app for you. A helper called the Concierge answers in plain words, and every page can be read aloud.

You can type or talk. The Concierge remembers what you tell it, sets reminders, adds events, and shows the photos and people you saved.

  • Ask for what you need in your own words.
  • One answer comes back at a time. Ask again to narrow it.
  • You decide who can help you, and you can stop their access at any time.

Getting started

Set up your account once, then use it every day.

This page never jumps into features before you are signed in. Sign in first, then fill in your name and place, then connect a calendar if you want one.

Sign in

There are two ways in. Both use the same account, and neither needs a password.

With an emailed link

  1. Open https://api.spirantix.ai/app.
  2. Type your email address.
  3. Choose Send my sign-in link.
  4. Open the email and tap the link.

If the email does not arrive, wait a minute, check the spam folder, then try once more.

With Google

  1. Choose Sign in with Google.
  2. Pick the Google account you want to use.
  3. That is it. You are signed in.

A helper may help more than one person. After signing in, People I help lists the people who shared access with you, and you choose whose account you are acting in.

Your first screens

  • The first screen asks who the account is for: Senior or Sponsor.
  • A senior is asked for a first name, so the app can greet them.
  • A senior is then asked where they live. A ZIP code is enough, or a city and state. We ask so the weather and the time are right for you.
  • A sponsor is not required. Many people live at home or with family.

Complete your profile

My account holds your name, your email address and your location. Change any of them at any time. Nothing here is shared with anyone unless you set up a helper.

Connect your calendar and other apps

Connecting Google lets your own appointments appear in the app.

  1. Choose Menu at the top right.
  2. Choose Connections.
  3. In the Google box, choose Connect Google.
  4. Pick the account you want to use.
  5. Google may say it has not verified the app. Choose Advanced, then continue.
  6. Choose which calendars Spirantix may use. One family calendar is usually enough.

To stop sharing, choose Disconnect Google in the same box.

Microsoft 365 and Yahoo appear under Connections and are marked Coming soon. Coming soon means you cannot connect it yet.

Using the Concierge app

The controls that work the same on every page.

These are basic features of the app, not extras. You will use them on every page, so they are gathered here once.

The menu

The Menu button is at the top right of every page. It lists every part of the app:

  • Home — the Concierge, and the next seven days
  • My to-do list — reminders
  • My people — the people you saved
  • My photos — photos and albums
  • My events — what is coming up
  • My helpers — who can help you
  • Connections — calendars and other apps
  • My account — your name, email and place

Sign out is at the bottom of the menu. A Support link sits in the footer of every page.

Asking the Concierge

The Concierge sits at the top of Home.

  • Chat answers from your memories. Ask about your own information.
  • Search looks on the web, safely, with no ads. It names its sources.
  1. Type your question.
  2. Choose Send.

Talking instead of typing

  • On Windows, hold the Windows key, the one with four squares, and press H.
  • On a Mac, turn on Dictation, then press the microphone key.
  • On a Chromebook, turn on dictation, then press Search and D.
  • On a phone or tablet, tap the microphone on the keyboard.
  • In Google Docs, choose Tools, then Voice typing.
  1. Tap the box where you would type.
  2. Start speaking.
  3. Check the words before you send them.

Read this page aloud

Read this page aloud sits at the very top of every page, above the header. Choose it to hear the page, and Stop reading to stop.

If you see Read aloud is not available on this device, this device cannot read pages aloud.

Home

The Concierge, the date, the weather, and the next seven days.

  • Ask the Concierge a question at the top of the page.
  • The next seven days run below it.
  • Tap a day to see that day in place.

My to-do list

Reminders, and nothing else.

  • Say “remind me” and the Concierge makes a reminder. It does not make an event.
  • A reminder appears on My to-do list, and on the day it is due.
  • A reminder is set, done, not done or expired. There is nothing else.
  • Only you mark a reminder done.

My people

The people you saved, with the names you gave them.

  • My people lists the people you have saved.
  • The names you save are the names the app uses.
  • The Concierge never guesses who is in a picture.

My photos

Pictures and the stories that go with them.

  • Photos and stories are kept in albums.
  • Ask for one photo and the Concierge shows one photo.
  • The names saved on a photo are the names you gave it.
  • If your button still says Photos, it is the same door.

My events

What is coming up, and what has already happened.

  • Scheduling something makes a calendar event.
  • Home shows the next seven days. Tap a day to see it in place.
  • My events lists what is coming up and what has already happened.
  • When Google is connected, the app names the calendar it used.

My helpers

Who can help you, and with what.

  • A family member or caregiver can help you, but only with your permission.
  • You choose what a helper can see, and you can stop their access at any time.
  • Helper management is always yours. A helper never sees this page.

My account

Your name, your email address and your location.

  • Change your name, email address or location at any time.
  • Connections and helpers are not on this page. They have their own menu entries.
  • Sign out is at the bottom of the menu.

When something goes wrong

What the app does, and how to reach a person.

  • An empty page says so and offers one next step.
  • A failed page says what happened in plain words.
  • If Google does not connect, try again from Connections.

Contact support

Email hello@spirantix.ai or use the support contact form. Describe what you were trying to do and what happened. Include your account email if it helps us find your account.

Never include a password, an account key, a sign-in link, or private family records. A screenshot is welcome if you cover anything private first.

Spirantix is not an emergency service.

Privacy and account deletion

To request correction or deletion of your Spirantix account information, contact support. We need to verify the account owner before making account changes. The privacy policy says what we store and what account deletion removes.
