Help with Spirantix Concierge

For help with your account, sign-in, a connected calendar, photos, reminders, or another Concierge feature, contact Spirantix.

Contact support

Email hello@spirantix.ai or use the support contact form. Describe what you were trying to do and what happened. Include your account email if it helps us find your account. Do not include a password, account key, sign-in link, or private family records.

Using the Concierge

Read the Concierge guide for example prompts and instructions for talking instead of typing.

Privacy and account deletion

To request correction or deletion of your Spirantix account information, contact support. We need to verify the account owner before making account changes. Read the privacy policy for what we store and what account deletion removes.

Before you send a request

A brief description of the problem and the name of the device or browser you are using can help. You can send a screenshot with private information removed. Spirantix is not an emergency service.
